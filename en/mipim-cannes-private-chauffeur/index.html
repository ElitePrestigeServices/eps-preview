MIPIM Cannes Private Chauffeur

MIPIM gathers real estate investors, developers and city delegations in Cannes every March. With back-to-back meetings at the Palais des Festivals, yacht receptions and dinners across the region, a private chauffeur lets you move between appointments without worrying about taxis or parking. Elite Prestige Services is based in Cannes and operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

From Nice Airport to Cannes

Your chauffeur tracks your flight and meets you in the arrivals hall of Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 at Nice Côte d’Azur Airport. In normal traffic the transfer to Cannes takes about 35 to 45 minutes; during MIPIM, the motorway exits and the town centre are busier, so plan a little margin before your first meeting. Private jet passengers can be collected at Cannes-Mandelieu Airport.

Access around the Palais des Festivals

Vehicle access around the Palais and along the Croisette is restricted during the event. Your chauffeur drops you at the nearest authorised point and arranges a clear pick-up location for the end of each meeting. Share your schedule in advance and we plan the timings around it.

A chauffeur at your disposal

Many delegates prefer an hourly booking for the busiest days: the car stays available for site visits, lunches outside Cannes, evening receptions and the late return to your hotel. It is also practical for teams hosting clients who need several pick-ups from different addresses.

Choosing your vehicle

Business Class (Mercedes E-Class or equivalent) seats up to 3 passengers and suits a solo traveller or a couple with standard luggage. Business Van (Mercedes V-Class) carries up to 7 passengers with room for large suitcases, golf bags or equipment. First Class (Mercedes S-Class or equivalent) is available on request for VIP guests and special occasions. Business Van is a popular choice for small delegations travelling together.

Price and booking

The price is fixed, per vehicle, and shown before you confirm your booking.

Frequently asked questions

Are you an official MIPIM transport provider?

No. Elite Prestige Services is independent and not affiliated with the organisers. Check the official dates and access rules before you travel.

Can you drive us to site visits outside Cannes?

Yes. Include all addresses in your request and you will receive a fixed price per vehicle, confirmed before booking.

What if my flight is delayed?

Your chauffeur follows your flight and adjusts to your actual landing time.
