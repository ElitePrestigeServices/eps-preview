MIPIM · Private chauffeur · Cannes

Private chauffeur for MIPIM in Cannes

For MIPIM, the international property market held in March at the Palais des Festivals, Elite Prestige Services, a private chauffeur company based in Cannes, handles your airport transfers, your meetings between the Palais, hotels and yachts, and your rides back from dinner. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and shown before you confirm your booking.

Book my MIPIM chauffeur

MIPIM, a week of meetings

For a few days, investors, developers, funds, architects and public authorities move between meetings on the stands, lunches in the Croisette hotels, appointments aboard yachts moored in the Vieux-Port and networking evenings. Diaries are planned to the half hour and change often. A chauffeur booked in advance saves you hunting for a taxi between meetings, at the times when the town is busiest.

Getting to Cannes for the event

From Nice Côte d’Azur Airport, allow about 35 minutes to Cannes outside peak traffic; during MIPIM, allow a margin, especially early in the morning and late in the afternoon. Your chauffeur tracks the actual landing time using your flight number and meets you at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2. See our Nice Airport – Cannes transfer.

Flying in privately? Cannes-Mandelieu Airport is about 10 to 20 minutes from the Palais des Festivals. See private aviation at Cannes-Mandelieu (in French).

A typical day with a chauffeur

In the morning, drop-off at the authorised point closest to the Palais. At midday, a business lunch in a Croisette hotel or on a private beach. In the afternoon, a visit to a property project in the hills above Cannes, in Mougins or at Sophia Antipolis. In the evening, drinks on a yacht, then dinner and back to your hotel. With hourly hire, on quotation, the same chauffeur stays with you all day: your documents stay in the car and your programme can change.

Delegations, local authorities and teams

A delegation of elected officials, a developer’s team or clients to host? The Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or equivalent) carries up to 7 people and 7 bags in a single vehicle. For a larger group, several vehicles are coordinated subject to availability, with a single point of contact and an invoice in your organisation’s name. See our corporate and events page.

Getting around the Palais des Festivals

During the event, the area around the Palais, the Croisette and the Vieux-Port is very busy and traffic arrangements change. Your chauffeur adapts drop-off points through the day and tells you in advance where to meet. Send a WhatsApp message a few minutes before a meeting ends and your chauffeur will be at the agreed point.

Available vehicles

Business Class: Mercedes E-Class / EQE or equivalent, 3 passengers and 3 bags. Business Van: Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or equivalent, 7 passengers and 7 bags. First Class: Mercedes S-Class / EQS or equivalent, 3 passengers, to host an investor or an executive. Electric Class: 100% electric EQE or EQS, for companies mindful of their carbon footprint.

Book early

Chauffeurs are in high demand during MIPIM. Send your programme as soon as your dates are known, via the booking form or WhatsApp, in English or French. You receive a fixed-price quote including VAT by email, with a secure card payment link. Official dates are listed in our events calendar.

Frequently asked questions

Can I keep the same chauffeur all day?

Yes, that is how hourly hire works, by the hour or by the day, on quotation.

Can you transport a delegation?

Yes, in a Business Van for up to 7 passengers, or with several coordinated vehicles.

Can the invoice be in our company’s name?

Yes, with each journey itemised.

Is the price per person?

No, it is per vehicle. It is fixed and shown before you confirm your booking.

Are you a MIPIM partner?

No. EPS is an independent transport company, not affiliated with the event organisers.
