Private chauffeur and driver service in Cannes

Elite Prestige Services is a private chauffeur company based in Cannes, listed on the French VTC private hire register (EVTC006240319). Available 24/7, by advance booking only, with a fixed-price quote per vehicle, VAT included.

A Cannes-based chauffeur, by reservation

Cannes is our home base. We know the access points to the Palais des Festivals, the hotel entrances along the Croisette, the narrow streets of Le Suquet and the quays of the Old Port. Give us an address, a time and the number of passengers, and we will email you a fixed price before your journey.

As a licensed private hire (VTC) operator, we do not pick up passengers on the street: every journey is booked in advance, through the website form or on WhatsApp. We reply in French or English.

Hotels, harbours and venues we serve in Cannes

We drop off and collect our clients outside the hotels on the Croisette, at residences in La Californie and La Croix-des-Gardes, at the Palais des Festivals, the Old Port and Port Canto. If you are heading out to sea, see also our page on transfers to French Riviera ports.

Cannes hotels and concierge services enjoy a fixed rate all year round, and a booking link in their own name.

At Cannes train station, the meeting point is the drop-off area at the top of the station: the forecourt in front of the main entrance is reserved for taxis, and private hire vehicles are fined there by CCTV. Give us your train number and your chauffeur will track its actual arrival time.

Film Festival, MIPIM, Cannes Lions: Cannes during major events

Every year Cannes hosts MIPIM, the Cannes Film Festival, Cannes Lions, the Yachting Festival and MIPCOM. On these dates, traffic around the Croisette is very heavy and chauffeurs get booked up early. We recommend booking as soon as your dates are confirmed.

Details on our Cannes Film Festival chauffeur page and in our events calendar.

Roadworks on the Croisette until 2028

Since mid-October 2026, the city of Cannes has been redeveloping the Croisette between rue Macé and boulevard Alexandre III. According to the announced schedule, a first phase runs until summer 2027 and a second from autumn 2027 to the end of 2028, with a break every May for the Cannes Film Festival. Traffic still flows west to east, but lanes shift as the works move and diversions are in place. Access to hotels, shops and residences is maintained.

In practice, your chauffeur checks the works on the day, picks the smoothest route, for instance via rue d’Antibes or boulevard Alexandre III, and drops you at your hotel entrance or the nearest permitted stop. Stopping is prohibited along the sections under works and enforced by cameras, so your chauffeur only stops at permitted points. For a meeting or an airport departure from the Croisette, allow a few extra minutes.

Good to know: the frontage of the Hôtel Martinez is also under works. Until spring 2027 (due to finish before the Cannes Film Festival), guests enter through the artists’ entrance at 17 rue Latour-Maubourg, and your chauffeur drops you off and picks you up at the nearest permitted stop to that entrance.

Christmas in Cannes 2026

The Christmas Village opens on the Allées de la Liberté and Cours Félix Faure from 27 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, with its chalets, light displays and ice rink. Facing the sea, the Palm Beach Christmas Village runs from 3 December 2026 to 3 January 2027, with free entry.

On New Year’s Eve, a drone show followed by a fireworks display is planned over the bay, themed on the 80th Cannes Film Festival. With the Croisette roadworks, we agree a drop-off and pick-up point in advance so you do not have to walk far after midnight.

Spending the festive season at a hotel on the Croisette? A chauffeur on standby by the hour lets you combine the Christmas markets, dinner and the fireworks without worrying about parking.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a chauffeur from Cannes to Nice Airport cost?

The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and shown before you confirm your booking. The drive takes about 35 minutes without traffic via the A8.

Can I book a chauffeur in Cannes at the last minute?

We work by advance booking only. Send your request as early as possible and we will confirm availability with your quote.

Is the price per person?

No, the price is per vehicle. A Business Van carries up to 7 passengers for a single price.

Do you offer night-time journeys?

Yes, the service runs 24/7, always by advance booking.

Can you take me to Saint-Tropez from Cannes?

Yes, as a direct transfer or with a chauffeur at your disposal for the day. Allow about 1 h 30 without traffic, often more in summer; the fixed price per vehicle is shown before you confirm. More on our Saint-Tropez private chauffeur page.
