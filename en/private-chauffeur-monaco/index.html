Private chauffeur and driver service in Monaco

Elite Prestige Services drives you around the Principality and from Monaco: Monte-Carlo, Port Hercule, Fontvieille, Larvotto. Prices start from 150 € incl. VAT to Nice Airport and from 230 € incl. VAT to Cannes, per vehicle. Chauffeur available 24/7, by advance booking, with a fixed-price quote.

Getting around Monaco with a chauffeur

Monaco is small but densely built: steep streets, tunnels, and hotel and residence entrances that can be tricky to reach. Your chauffeur drops you outside the Casino de Monte-Carlo, on the quayside at Port Hercule, in Fontvieille or at your building, then comes back to collect you at the agreed time.

For an evening out, a series of meetings or a day of sightseeing, a chauffeur at your disposal is available on request.

Popular journeys from Monaco

Prices from, VAT included, per vehicle (Business Class / Business Van / First Class):

Monaco ↔ Nice Côte d’Azur Airport: 150 € / 195 € / 240 €, about 30 min without traffic.

Grand Prix, Yacht Show: Monaco during major events

During the Monaco Grand Prix, part of the city is closed to traffic and access routes change from one day to the next. We plan your journeys around the access points and your schedule; booking early is strongly advised. See our Monaco Grand Prix chauffeur page.

In September, the Monaco Yacht Show takes over Port Hercule: we provide transfers between the airport, your hotel and the show. Only the higher of the two applies, never both.

Hotels, residences and yachts

We work with hotels, concierge services and agencies across the Principality: a fixed rate all year round, and a booking link in the establishment’s name. Find out more on our hotels and concierge services page.

Boarding or leaving a yacht? We will meet you at the harbour with your luggage. Details on our port transfers.

Saloon, van or S-Class in Monaco

First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or similar, 3 passengers) is a popular choice for an evening at the casino or a business meeting. Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or similar, 3 passengers, 3 pieces of luggage) suits everyday journeys. The Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or similar, 7 passengers, 7 pieces of luggage) is ideal for a group or for the trip back from a cruise. Electric Class (100% electric Mercedes EQE / EQS): same price as Business Class for the EQE and as First Class for the EQS. Baby seats and booster seats available on request.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a chauffeur from Monaco to Nice Airport cost?

Can you drive during the Grand Prix?

Yes, taking the road closures into account. We adjust the pick-up points; please book as early as possible.

Do you offer a chauffeur at your disposal in Monaco?

Yes, for an evening or a full day, price on request.

Do you drive to Italy from Monaco?

Yes, long-distance journeys to Italy are available on request.

Can I book a late-night journey?

Yes, the service runs 24/7, by advance booking.
