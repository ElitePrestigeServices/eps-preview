Private chauffeur and driver service in Cannes

Elite Prestige Services is a private chauffeur company based in Cannes, listed on the French VTC private hire register (EVTC006240319). We drive you from the Croisette, Le Suquet, Port Canto or your villa to Nice Airport (from 130 €), Cannes-Mandelieu Airport (from 95 €), Monaco or Saint-Tropez. Available 24/7, by advance booking only, with a fixed-price quote per vehicle, VAT included.

A Cannes-based chauffeur, by reservation

Cannes is our home base. We know the access points to the Palais des Festivals, the hotel entrances along the Croisette, the narrow streets of Le Suquet and the quays of the Old Port. Give us an address, a time and the number of passengers, and we will email you a fixed price before your journey.

As a licensed private hire (VTC) operator, we do not pick up passengers on the street: every journey is booked in advance, through the website form or on WhatsApp. We reply in French or English.

Popular journeys from Cannes and starting prices

Prices from, VAT included, per vehicle and not per person (Business Class / Business Van / First Class):

Cannes ↔ Nice Côte d’Azur Airport: 130 € / 160 € / 200 €, about 35 min without traffic via the A8.

Cannes ↔ Cannes-Mandelieu Airport: 95 € / 115 € / 120 €, for private flights and business aviation.

Cannes ↔ Monaco: 230 € / 260 € / 330 €, about 55 min without traffic.

Cannes ↔ Saint-Tropez: 320 € / 370 € / 460 €, about 1 hr 30 min without traffic.

Antibes, Juan-les-Pins, Mougins, Cannes railway station or long-distance journeys to Marseille, Toulon or Italy: price on request. In July and August, prices are increased by 15%.

Hotels, harbours and venues we serve in Cannes

We drop off and collect our clients outside the hotels on the Croisette, at residences in La Californie and La Croix-des-Gardes, at the Palais des Festivals, the Old Port and Port Canto. If you are heading out to sea, see also our page on transfers to French Riviera ports.

Cannes hotels and concierge services enjoy a fixed rate all year round, and a booking link in their own name.

Film Festival, MIPIM, Cannes Lions: Cannes during major events

Every year Cannes hosts MIPIM, the Cannes Film Festival, Cannes Lions, the Yachting Festival and MIPCOM. On these dates, traffic around the Croisette is very heavy and chauffeurs get booked up early. We recommend booking as soon as your dates are confirmed.

Details on our Cannes Film Festival chauffeur page and in our events calendar.

Our vehicles in Cannes

Business Class: Mercedes E-Class / EQE or similar, 3 passengers and 3 pieces of luggage. Business Van: Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or similar, 7 passengers and 7 pieces of luggage, ideal for a family or a team. First Class: Mercedes S-Class / EQS or similar, 3 passengers. Electric Class (100% electric Mercedes EQE / EQS): same price as Business Class for the EQE and as First Class for the EQS. Baby seats and booster seats available on request.

How to book your chauffeur in Cannes

Tell us the pick-up address, destination, date, time, number of passengers and pieces of luggage, plus your flight or train number if you have one. We reply by email with a fixed-price quote including VAT; payment is then made by card via a secure Stripe link.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a chauffeur from Cannes to Nice Airport cost?

From 130 € incl. VAT in Business Class, 160 € in Business Van and 200 € in First Class, per vehicle.

Can I book a chauffeur in Cannes at the last minute?

We work by advance booking only. Send your request as early as possible and we will confirm availability with your quote.

Is the price per person?

No, the price is per vehicle. A Business Van carries up to 7 passengers for a single price.

Do you offer night-time journeys?

Yes, the service runs 24/7, always by advance booking.

Can you take me to Saint-Tropez from Cannes?

Yes, from 320 € in Business Class, for a drive of about 1 hr 30 min without traffic.
