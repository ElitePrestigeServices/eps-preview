Private chauffeur and driver service in Monaco

Need a chauffeur in Monaco? Elite Prestige Services handles your transfers between Nice Airport and the Principality, your journeys around Monte-Carlo and your trips to Cannes, Èze, Menton or Italy. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and shown before you confirm your booking. Chauffeur available 24/7, by advance booking only.

Why a private chauffeur in the Principality

Monaco covers a very small area, built in terraces between the sea and the mountains: steep streets, tunnels, one-way systems, and hotel and residence entrances that can be hard to find. Your chauffeur knows these access points and drops you outside the Casino de Monte-Carlo, on the quayside at Port Hercule, in Fontvieille, at Larvotto or at the foot of your building.

Rather than waiting for a taxi after dinner or sharing a shuttle from the airport, you book a private hire vehicle in advance: the car is for you alone, the price is known before you set off and the chauffeur arrives at the agreed time.

Popular journeys to and from Monaco

Typical times, without traffic:

Nice Côte d’Azur Airport ↔ Monaco: about 30 minutes. Every step of the meet-and-greet at the terminal is described on our Nice Airport – Monaco transfer page.

Cannes ↔ Monaco: about 55 minutes via the A8.

Èze, Menton, Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat, central Nice: pick-up at the address of your choice, with a fixed price shown before you confirm. Depending on the time of day, your chauffeur takes the A8 or the Moyenne Corniche.

Saint-Tropez, Italy, Marseille: long-distance journeys priced on request.

Business meetings, conferences and evenings out

For a day of meetings, the chauffeur can stay at your disposal and take you from one appointment to the next, then to the airport at the end of the day: priced on request. Conference or seminar delegates can be collected at the airport and dropped directly at the venue: see our Monaco conference chauffeur page.

In the evening, book a return trip for dinner or a night at the casino. You give us the time you would like to come back; if it changes, just let your chauffeur know on WhatsApp.

Monaco during major events

The Monaco Grand Prix closes part of the streets for several days and changes the drop-off points. We plan your journeys around the open access routes and your schedule: Monaco Grand Prix chauffeur. The Monte-Carlo Masters, in April at the Monte-Carlo Country Club on the edge of the Principality, and the Monaco Yacht Show, in September at Port Hercule, also draw many visitors. For these periods, book as early as possible.

Hotels, residences and yachts

Hotels, concierge services and agencies in the Principality can book for their guests at a fixed rate all year round, with a booking link in their own name: details on our hotels and concierge services page. Boarding a yacht at Port Hercule or coming back from one? Tell us the berth and the name of the boat: your chauffeur will be waiting with your luggage.

The right vehicle for your journey

First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or similar, 3 passengers) suits an evening out or an important meeting. Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or similar, 3 passengers, 3 pieces of luggage) covers most transfers. The Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or similar, 7 passengers, 7 pieces of luggage) takes a family or a team for a single price. Electric Class (100% electric EQE or EQS) is offered at the Business Class rate for the EQE and the First Class rate for the EQS. Baby seats and booster seats on request.

Book in a few minutes

Give us your pick-up address, destination, date, time, number of passengers and pieces of luggage, and your flight number if you are arriving at Nice Airport: the chauffeur tracks the actual landing time. Frequently asked questions

How long is the drive from Nice Airport to Monaco?

About 30 minutes without traffic, longer at rush hour and during major events.

Is the price per person?

No. The price is fixed, per vehicle, and shown before you confirm your booking.

Can you drive during the Grand Prix?

Yes, taking the closed streets into account. Pick-up points are adjusted; please book as early as possible.

Can I keep the chauffeur for a whole day?

Yes, a chauffeur at your disposal for half a day or a full day is available, priced on request.

Do you drive to Italy from Monaco?

Yes, journeys to Italy are available, priced on request.
