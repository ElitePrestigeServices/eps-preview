Private chauffeur and driver service in Nice

Need a chauffeur in Nice, from Nice Côte d’Azur Airport, Nice-Ville station or your hotel on the Promenade des Anglais? Elite Prestige Services, a private hire (VTC) company based in Cannes, drives you anywhere on the French Riviera, 24/7 on advance booking. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and is shown before you confirm your booking.

Pick-up at Nice Côte d’Azur Airport

The airport lies west of the city, right by the sea. We meet our clients at Terminal 1 and Terminal 2. With your flight number, the chauffeur tracks your actual landing time: whether your flight is early or late, he is there when you come out and takes care of your luggage. For business aviation, the meeting point is agreed in advance, see private aviation at Nice Airport.

Journey times from Nice Airport

Indicative times, outside traffic: Antibes about 20 to 30 min; Èze and Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat about 25 to 35 min; Monaco about 30 min; Cannes about 35 min; Menton about 40 to 55 min; Saint-Tropez about 1 hr 30 to 2 hrs. The most requested routes have their own detailed page: Nice Airport – Cannes and Nice – Monaco. Long distances to Marseille, Toulon or Italy are on quotation.

Neighbourhoods and addresses we serve

Promenade des Anglais: seafront hotels, drop-off at the entrance. Old Nice and Cours Saleya: narrow, pedestrian streets, so the chauffeur drops you as close as possible and tells you the pick-up point. Carré d’Or and city centre: shops, hotels and apartments. Port of Nice: boat departures and quayside restaurants. Cimiez and Mont Boron: villas and residences on the hills, often with private access. Nice-Ville station: give us your train number and the chauffeur tracks its actual arrival time.

Nice for business and events

Nice hosts conferences, concerts and sporting fixtures, notably at the Acropolis convention centre and the Allianz Riviera stadium. On busy evenings, booking a chauffeur saves you looking for parking and waiting at the exit. Access and pick-up points are detailed on our chauffeur for events in Nice page. Companies and agencies can receive an invoice in their company’s name.

Taxi, shuttle or private hire: what to choose in Nice?

At the airport and in town, several means of transport exist side by side: taxis at the ranks, the tram, buses and shuttles to nearby towns. They suit a one-off trip without luggage. A pre-booked private hire car meets a different need: a chauffeur waiting for you by name, no changes on the way, luggage loaded for you and a price known before you set off. For a family, a group or a traveller tired after a long flight, it is often the simplest solution.

Exploring the Riviera from Nice

Nice is a good base for sightseeing on the French Riviera. Along the Moyenne Corniche you reach the hilltop village of Èze and then Monaco, with its gardens, the Casino de Monte-Carlo and Port Hercule. To the west, Antibes, Saint-Paul-de-Vence or Grasse can be visited in half a day. These excursions are booked as chauffeur hire, on quotation: ideas for tours on our excursions with a chauffeur on the French Riviera page.

Which vehicle for Nice?

Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or similar, 3 passengers, 3 pieces of luggage) suits a solo traveller or a couple. The Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or similar, 7 passengers, 7 pieces of luggage) takes a family with a pushchair or a group with golf bags. First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or similar, 3 passengers) is for journeys where comfort comes first. Electric Class (100% electric Mercedes EQE / EQS) is charged at the Business Class rate in an EQE and the First Class rate in an EQS. Baby seats and booster seats on request.
