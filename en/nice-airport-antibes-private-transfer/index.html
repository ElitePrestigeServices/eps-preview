Private transfer · Nice Airport · Antibes

Nice Airport to Antibes private transfer with chauffeur

From Nice Côte d’Azur Airport to Antibes, Juan-les-Pins or Cap d’Antibes, your private chauffeur meets you as you come out of arrivals and drops you at your door in about 20 to 30 minutes outside peak traffic. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and shown before you confirm your booking. Service available 24/7, by advance booking only.

Book my transfer to Antibes

Your arrival, step by step

When booking, you give us your flight number, the number of passengers and bags, and your exact address in Antibes. On the day, your chauffeur tracks the actual landing time: if your flight is delayed, they simply adjust, with no need for you to call. On arrival, they wait for you in the arrivals hall at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2, take your luggage and walk you to the car. On the road, the A8 or the coast road depending on the time of day, then straight to your address.

Journey times by destination

Antibes town centre and Port Vauban: about 20 to 30 minutes. Juan-les-Pins: about 25 to 35 minutes. Cap d’Antibes: a little longer, depending on where your villa or hotel is on the peninsula. In summer, traffic along the seafront and around the pine grove can make the journey noticeably longer; the price agreed at booking does not change.

Where we drop you

In the old town, several streets are pedestrianised or have restricted access: you are dropped at the nearest authorised point to your accommodation, with help for your luggage. For a villa on Cap d’Antibes, please send us the gate code or the caretaker’s contact. To join a boat at Port Vauban, give us the quay, the yacht’s name and the time agreed with the crew: see also our port and yacht transfers (in French).

The return to the airport

Tell us your return flight time and we will agree the pick-up time together, with a margin suited to the traffic at that moment. Booking both legs at once means one less thing to think about during your stay. The service also covers very early morning and late-night flights.

Families, groups and crews

Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or equivalent) seats 3 passengers with 3 bags. Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or equivalent) takes up to 7 passengers and 7 bags for a single price: pushchairs, kit bags and large suitcases included within the vehicle’s capacity. First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or equivalent, 3 passengers) and Electric Class (100% electric EQE or EQS) are also available. Baby seats and booster seats on request.

And during your stay?

Once you have settled in, your chauffeur can take you to dinner in Juan-les-Pins, for a day in Cannes or Monaco, or to a Jazz à Juan concert in July. Ideas are on our page on a private chauffeur in Antibes (in French). Staying in Cannes instead? See our Nice Airport – Cannes transfer.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the transfer from Nice Airport to Antibes?

About 20 to 30 minutes to Antibes town centre outside peak traffic, a little longer for Juan-les-Pins and Cap d’Antibes.

Will the chauffeur wait if my flight is late?

Yes, they track the actual arrival time using your flight number.

Is the price per person?

No, it is per vehicle. It is fixed and shown before you confirm your booking.

Can you drop me in Juan-les-Pins?

Yes. Juan-les-Pins is part of Antibes: just give us the exact address of your accommodation.

Is the transfer available at night?

Yes, 24/7, by advance booking.
