Private transfer from Nice Airport to Cannes

Elite Prestige Services provides your private transfer between Nice Côte d’Azur Airport and Cannes in about 35 minutes without traffic. Your chauffeur tracks your flight, meets you at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 and drops you at your hotel, on the Croisette or at your villa. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and is shown before you confirm your booking.

Book my transfer

The transfer at a glance

Pick-up at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2, a drive along the A8, arrival at your address in Cannes: about 35 minutes without traffic. The service runs 24/7, by advance booking, for an early morning flight as well as a late evening arrival. The same journey can be booked the other way for your departure.

A welcome timed to your flight

When booking, give us your flight number and, if you know it, the terminal. The chauffeur follows your actual landing time: early or late, the pick-up is adjusted without you having to let us know. The exact meeting arrangements are sent with your confirmation. You collect your luggage, meet your chauffeur and the car heads straight for Cannes.

Where we drop you in Cannes

We cover every address in Cannes: the hotels on the Croisette, the Palais des Festivals, Le Suquet, La Californie, the Old Port, Port Canto, flats in the town centre and villas in the hills. For a villa, send us the exact address and, if there is one, the gate code. Staying in Le Cannet, Mougins or Mandelieu-la-Napoule? The transfer is booked in exactly the same way: see also our private chauffeur in Mougins page.

Trade shows and festivals: allowing for traffic

During the Cannes Film Festival, MIPIM, Cannes Lions or the Yachting Festival, parts of the Croisette are often closed and the A8 is busier. The price stays fixed however long the drive takes; your chauffeur adapts the route and drops you at the access point closest to your hotel. Book as soon as your dates are known: see our Cannes Film Festival chauffeur page.

Back to Nice Airport

For your departure, tell us your flight time: together we set the pick-up time in Cannes, with a margin suited to the day and the season. The chauffeur drops you in front of the right terminal, as close as possible to check-in.

Families, groups and luggage

Travelling with children? Tell us how many baby seats or booster seats you need: they are provided on request. From 4 passengers, or with lots of suitcases, pushchairs or golf bags, choose the Business Van: up to 7 passengers and 7 pieces of luggage, for a single price per vehicle.

Choosing your vehicle

Business Class: Mercedes E-Class / EQE or similar, 3 passengers and 3 pieces of luggage. Business Van: Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or similar, 7 passengers and 7 pieces of luggage. First Class: Mercedes S-Class / EQS or similar, 3 passengers. Electric Class: 100% electric Mercedes EQE or EQS, at the Business Class price for the EQE and the First Class price for the EQS.

Private jet: Nice or Cannes-Mandelieu

Is your business jet landing at Cannes-Mandelieu Airport? The transfer to Cannes is then much shorter: see our private aviation at Cannes-Mandelieu page.

Taxi, shuttle or private chauffeur from Nice Airport to Cannes?

For this journey you can take a taxi from the rank, a shuttle or a bus, or book a private hire chauffeur in advance. A private transfer means no taxi queue and none of the intermediate stops of a shuttle: the chauffeur is waiting for you, handles your luggage and drives you straight to your address. With several travellers, the price per vehicle stays the same, whether you are in Cannes on holiday, for a trade show or for a hotel stay.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a transfer from Nice Airport to Cannes cost?

It is a fixed price for the vehicle, not per passenger: you see it in the booking form before you confirm.

How long does the journey take?

About 35 minutes without traffic via the A8, longer at peak times or during major trade shows.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

The chauffeur tracks your actual arrival time using your flight number and adjusts accordingly.

Does the price depend on the number of passengers?

No, it depends on the vehicle. Up to 7 passengers travel in the Business Van for a single price.

Can I book for a flight arriving at night?

Yes, the service runs 24/7, by advance booking.
