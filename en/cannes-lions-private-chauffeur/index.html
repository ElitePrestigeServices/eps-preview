Private chauffeur for Cannes Lions

For Cannes Lions, the international festival of creativity that brings agencies, advertisers and media to the Palais des Festivals every June, Elite Prestige Services, a private chauffeur company based in Cannes, arranges your transfers, your client meetings and your rides home after the parties. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and shown before you confirm your booking.

Book my Cannes Lions chauffeur

A week of talks and evenings

During Cannes Lions, days start early at the Palais and end late on the beaches of the Croisette, where brands and platforms host their guests. Between two talks, a client lunch, an awards ceremony and a beach party, there are many journeys to make, and demand for cars is very high in the evening. With a chauffeur booked in advance, you never queue for a taxi.

Transfers from Nice Airport

Allow about 35 minutes between Nice Côte d’Azur Airport and Cannes outside peak traffic, longer at rush hour during the festival. Your chauffeur tracks your flight, meets you at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 and takes you to your hotel in Cannes, Le Cannet, Mougins or Antibes. For your departure, we set the pick-up time around your flight with a suitable margin. See our Nice Airport – Cannes transfer.

Agencies, advertisers and delegations

Bringing over a team, clients or speakers? The Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or equivalent) carries up to 7 people and 7 bags in one journey. Several vehicles can be coordinated for the same delegation, subject to availability, with a single point of contact and an invoice in your company’s name. Details are on our corporate and events page.

Hourly hire, ideal during the festival

Hourly hire, on quotation, keeps the same chauffeur with you for half a day, a full day or the whole week. They drop you at a talk, collect you for lunch, take you to a reception in a villa in the hills and bring you back after the last party. Your belongings and client gifts stay in the car, and the programme can change from one hour to the next.

Traffic on the Croisette

In the evening, the Croisette and the area around the Palais are very busy and some sections are restricted. Your chauffeur knows the drop-off points that work and adapts the route to the closures of the day. For beach parties, agree a pick-up point and time, or send a WhatsApp message a few minutes before you leave. Staying in Mougins? See our page on a private chauffeur in Mougins (in French).

Available vehicles

Business Class: Mercedes E-Class / EQE or equivalent, 3 passengers and 3 bags. Business Van: Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or equivalent, 7 passengers and 7 bags. First Class: Mercedes S-Class / EQS or equivalent, 3 passengers, to welcome a key client. Electric Class: 100% electric EQE or EQS.

Booking for Cannes Lions

Availability goes quickly in June: book as soon as your dates are known, via the booking form or WhatsApp, in English or French. You receive a fixed-price quote including VAT by email, with a secure card payment link. Official dates are listed in our events calendar.

Frequently asked questions

Can we book a chauffeur for the whole week?

Yes, we prepare a quotation based on your schedule and the number of days.

Can you invoice our agency?

Yes, the invoice is made out in your company’s name, with each journey itemised.

Can the chauffeur wait for us after a beach party?

Yes, with hourly hire or a pick-up time agreed in advance.

Is the price per person?

No, it is per vehicle. It is fixed and shown before you confirm your booking.

Are you connected with the organisers?

No. EPS is an independent transport company, not affiliated with the Cannes Lions organisers.
