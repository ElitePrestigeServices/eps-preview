Corporate and event chauffeur service on the French Riviera

Are you organising the arrival of guests, executives, teams or talent between Nice Airport, Cannes, Monaco and Saint-Tropez? Elite Prestige Services, a private hire (VTC) company based in Cannes, handles your transfers and chauffeur hire 24/7, on advance booking. Each journey has a fixed price per vehicle, confirmed before the assignment, and the invoice is issued in your company’s name.

What we cover

Airport meet and greet: your guests are met at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 of Nice Côte d’Azur, or at Cannes-Mandelieu for business aviation, with flight tracking. Hotel – venue transfers: to and from a dinner, a brand evening or a conference. Chauffeur at disposal: a dedicated chauffeur for half a day, a full day or the whole length of a trade show, on quotation. Business appointments: site visits, meetings in Monaco or Nice, an executive travelling between two cities.

Who is it for?

Event agencies and DMCs: you need a reliable ground transport provider, for a full programme or as back-up at peak times. Production companies and brands: your talent’s schedules change often; we follow your timetable throughout the day. Companies: board members, guest clients, a foreign delegation or a sales team travelling on the Riviera. Hotels and concierge services: your business guests receive the same care, see hotels and concierges.

Trade shows, conferences and festivals

The French Riviera calendar is shaped by major professional gatherings: MIPIM, the Cannes Film Festival, Cannes Lions, the Cannes Yachting Festival, MIPCOM, TFWA, ILTM, as well as the Grand Prix and the Monaco Yacht Show. During these weeks, traffic around the Palais des Festivals and Port Hercule changes: closed streets, restricted drop-off areas and strong demand when evenings end. Our guides cover each event; the easiest starting point is the French Riviera events calendar. Book as soon as your dates are set.

Seminars and meetings

For a seminar in Cannes, we organise participants’ arrivals from the airport or the station, shuttles to the meeting venue and evening outings. Details are on our seminars in Cannes page. In Monaco, conferences and business meetings follow the same approach: one programme, one contact, one fixed price per journey.

An assignment, from quote to invoice

1. The programme: you send the list of passengers, flights or trains, addresses and times. 2. Confirmation: we come back to you with a fixed price per vehicle, or an overall quotation for chauffeur hire. 3. Preparation: each journey has a booking order, as French VTC regulations require, and the chauffeur receives all the details in advance. 4. On the day: a direct WhatsApp contact for any change of time or passenger. 5. Afterwards: an invoice in your company’s name, with each journey itemised.

The vehicles

Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or similar, 3 passengers, 3 pieces of luggage) for an executive or a guest. Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or similar, 7 passengers, 7 pieces of luggage) for a small team or a talent and their entourage. First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or similar, 3 passengers) for your guests of honour. Electric Class (100% electric Mercedes EQE / EQS) if your event favours electric mobility. If you need several vehicles at the same time, say so in your request: we will confirm what can be provided.

Discretion and presentation

Our chauffeurs drive executives and well-known personalities: no information about your passengers or their movements is shared, and no photos are published. Rather than a taxi picked up at random from a rank, your guests are met on time by a chauffeur who knows their name and their schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Can you work alongside our usual provider?

Yes. We can take on part of the journeys during the peaks of a trade show or festival, according to your schedule.

Can the invoice be in the company’s name?

Yes. Give your company name and billing address in your request; each journey is itemised.

How is the price calculated?

Each transfer has a fixed price per vehicle, not per person, confirmed before the assignment. Chauffeur hire and long distances are on quotation.

How far ahead should we book?

For a major event, as soon as your dates are known. Otherwise, a request the day before is often enough.

Do your chauffeurs speak English?

We answer in French and English, and regularly welcome international passengers.
