Nice Airport to Èze Private Transfer

Perched high above the Mediterranean between Nice and Monaco, Èze is one of the most spectacular stops on the Riviera. Our private transfer takes you straight from Nice Côte d’Azur Airport to Èze Village, Èze-sur-Mer or your hotel or villa nearby, with a professional chauffeur and a fixed price confirmed before booking.

Book your transfer to Èze →

Meeting you at Nice Airport

Your chauffeur tracks your flight in real time and waits for you in the arrivals hall of Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 at Nice Côte d’Azur Airport, with a name sign if you wish. If your flight is early or delayed, the pick-up time adjusts automatically, and help with luggage is included. In normal traffic, the drive to Èze takes about 25 to 35 minutes, depending on whether you stay in the village or by the sea.

Èze Village and Èze-sur-Mer

The medieval village is closed to cars: its lanes are pedestrian-only, so your chauffeur drops you at the nearest accessible point or at your hotel entrance. Èze-sur-Mer, on the coast below, is reached by the lower road. Share your full address and any access instructions so the route can be planned precisely.

Beyond the transfer

Èze is a natural base for visiting Monaco, Beaulieu-sur-Mer, Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat and Villefranche. Book a chauffeur by the hour for a day along the three Corniche roads, a dinner in Monte-Carlo or a shopping trip to Nice.

Choosing your vehicle

Business Class (Mercedes E-Class or equivalent) seats up to 3 passengers and suits a solo traveller or a couple with standard luggage. Business Van (Mercedes V-Class) carries up to 7 passengers with room for large suitcases, golf bags or equipment. First Class (Mercedes S-Class or equivalent) is available on request for VIP guests and special occasions. Tell us the number of suitcases so we choose the right size.

Price

Every transfer to Èze is quoted at a fixed price per vehicle, not per person, confirmed before booking. There are no meter surprises in traffic. In high season (July and August) and during major events, a demand-related surcharge applies; the price is still confirmed before you book.

Frequently asked questions

Can you drive into Èze Village?

No vehicle can enter the historic centre. We drop you as close as the road allows.

Can I book the return journey to the airport?

Yes. Add the return date and time in the same request; your chauffeur collects you at your address and leaves enough margin for check-in.

Do you operate early in the morning or late at night?

Yes. Elite Prestige Services runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including for late arrivals.
