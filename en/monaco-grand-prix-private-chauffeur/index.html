Monaco Grand Prix · Private chauffeur · Monaco

Private chauffeur for the Monaco Grand Prix

For the Monaco Grand Prix, in late May or early June, Elite Prestige Services arranges your transfer from Nice Airport, Cannes or your hotel on the French Riviera, as well as hourly hire throughout the race weekend. Private chauffeur service by advance booking: the price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and shown before you confirm your booking.

Book my Grand Prix chauffeur

Monaco becomes a racetrack

During practice, qualifying and the race, much of the Principality’s streets become the circuit: grandstands around Port Hercule, controlled access around the Casino de Monte-Carlo, yachts moored along the track and receptions late into the night. Traffic is heavily restricted and drop-off points are set by the authorities. Your chauffeur plans the route around these restrictions and drops you at the authorised point closest to your grandstand, hotel or boat.

Journey times and departure time

Outside the event, allow about 30 minutes between Nice Côte d’Azur Airport and Monaco, and about 55 minutes from Cannes. During the Grand Prix, these times increase significantly, especially before and after track sessions. Your chauffeur chooses between the A8 and the Moyenne Corniche depending on traffic and recommends a departure time with a comfortable margin.

Arriving by plane? Give us your flight number: your chauffeur tracks the actual landing time and meets you at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2. Route details are on our Nice Airport to Monaco transfer page.

Rather than a packed train or a taxi queue

On race days, coastal trains are packed, shuttles stop far from the grandstands and taxis are hard to find at the exit. A chauffeur booked in advance drops you off and picks you up at the agreed point, with a price known from the moment you book. You can leave your belongings in the car and there is no parking to find in a city closed to traffic.

Staying in Nice, Cannes, Èze or Menton

Many spectators stay outside the Principality and travel in and out each day. With a morning drop-off and a return at an agreed time, you enjoy the sessions without worrying about the journey. Your chauffeur collects you at the point you agreed together, even late after an evening on a yacht or in Monte-Carlo. For a stay in Monaco itself, see our private chauffeur in Monaco page.

Hourly hire over the weekend

To move between grandstand, lunch, reception and evening, hourly hire, on quotation, keeps the same chauffeur and the same vehicle with you. They stay reachable by phone or WhatsApp and meet you at the agreed pick-up point as soon as traffic allows. For a company hosting clients or an agency managing guests, several vehicles can be coordinated, subject to availability: see our corporate and events page.

Available vehicles

Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or similar, 3 passengers, 3 bags). Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or similar, 7 passengers, 7 bags), ideal for a group of friends or guests. First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or similar, 3 passengers) to host a client. Electric Class (fully electric EQE or EQS) on request. Baby seats and boosters on request.

Frequently asked questions

Can you drop me right at my grandstand?

Your chauffeur drops you at the closest authorised point; direct drop-offs depend on the day’s road closures.

How long from Nice Airport to Monaco during the Grand Prix?

About 30 minutes outside the event, significantly longer on race days.

Can the chauffeur wait until the end of the evening?

Yes, with hourly hire on quotation, for a return without waiting.

Is the price per person?

No, it is per vehicle. It is fixed and shown before you confirm your booking.

Are you connected with the Grand Prix organisers?

No. EPS is an independent transport company, not affiliated with the organisers.
