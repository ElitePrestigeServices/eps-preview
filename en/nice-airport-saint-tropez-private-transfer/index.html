Nice Airport to Saint-Tropez private transfer with chauffeur

Landing in Nice and staying in Saint-Tropez, Ramatuelle or Gassin? Elite Prestige Services drives you from Nice Côte d’Azur Airport to your hotel, villa or the harbour in about 1 hr 30 to 2 hrs, depending on traffic. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and shown before you confirm your booking.

The route from Nice to Saint-Tropez

Saint-Tropez is about 100 km from the airport. Your chauffeur takes the A8 westbound, then the roads of the peninsula into the Gulf of Saint-Tropez. Allow about 1 hr 30 to 2 hrs in normal conditions. At the height of summer and in the late afternoon, the road into the gulf is very busy and the journey can exceed 2 hrs 30. Your chauffeur adapts the route to the time of day and the traffic, and the price agreed at booking does not change if the road is slower than expected.

Meet and greet at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2

Give us your flight number: your chauffeur tracks the actual landing time and adjusts if your flight is delayed. You are met in the arrivals hall, your luggage is taken care of and you are settled into the car. A stop on the way, for lunch or some shopping, can be arranged on request when you book. Arriving by private jet? See our page on private aviation at Nice Airport (in French).

Every address in the gulf

Saint-Tropez: the harbour, the Place des Lices, the Citadel and the hotels in the centre. Ramatuelle: the Pampelonne beaches and the villas of the peninsula. Gassin, Grimaud, Port Grimaud, Cogolin and Sainte-Maxime: hotels, estates and residences. Please give the exact address when booking: many properties have private lanes or a coded gate.

The return to the airport

For your departure, tell us your flight time and we will agree a pick-up time together, with a comfortable margin to get out of the gulf. Booking both legs at once gives you peace of mind, especially in July and August when requests are numerous.

Les Voiles de Saint-Tropez and summer stays

During Les Voiles de Saint-Tropez in the autumn, the harbour and its surroundings are very busy. In summer, the beach restaurants of Pampelonne and evenings on the peninsula draw large crowds. For getting around once you have arrived, see our page on a private chauffeur in Saint-Tropez; for the sailing regatta, our page on a chauffeur for Les Voiles de Saint-Tropez (in French).

Saloon or van?

Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or equivalent) seats 3 passengers with 3 bags. Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or equivalent) takes up to 7 passengers and 7 bags: the right choice for a family on holiday or a group of friends. First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or equivalent, 3 passengers) offers the most comfort on this long drive. Electric Class (100% electric EQE or EQS) is also available. Baby seats and booster seats on request.

Booking your transfer

Use the booking form or WhatsApp, in English or French: date, flight number, number of passengers and bags, address in the gulf. You receive a fixed-price quote including VAT by email, with a secure card payment link. Service available 24/7, by advance booking only.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the drive from Nice Airport to Saint-Tropez?

About 1 hr 30 to 2 hrs in normal conditions, longer at the height of summer and in the late afternoon.

Does the price change if traffic makes the journey longer?

No. The price is fixed, per vehicle, and shown before you confirm your booking.

Will the chauffeur wait if my flight is late?

Yes, your chauffeur tracks the actual arrival time using your flight number.

Can we stop on the way?

Yes, on request when you book.

Do you also drive from Cannes to Saint-Tropez?
