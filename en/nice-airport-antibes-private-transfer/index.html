Nice Airport – Antibes private transfer with chauffeur

Landing at Nice Côte d’Azur Airport and staying in Antibes, Juan-les-Pins or on the Cap d’Antibes? Your private chauffeur meets you as you leave the terminal and drops you at your door in about 20 to 30 minutes without traffic. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and shown before you confirm your booking. Service available 24/7, by advance booking only.

Book my transfer to Antibes

How your arrival works

When booking, you give us your flight number, the number of passengers and pieces of luggage, and the exact address of your accommodation. On the day, the chauffeur tracks the actual landing time: if your flight is delayed, he adjusts without you having to let him know.

He waits for you in the arrivals hall of Terminal 1 or Terminal 2, helps with your suitcases and walks you to the vehicle. Depending on the time and traffic, he takes the A8 or the coast road, then drops you directly at the agreed address, with no stops on the way.

Private hire, taxi or shuttle: what changes

At Nice Airport you will also find taxis and shuttles. A private hire transfer booked in advance has several advantages: the price is set before you travel and does not depend on traffic jams, the vehicle is reserved for your party alone, and the chauffeur is already waiting when you arrive. With a shuttle, stops are shared and part of the journey is often left to do with your luggage.

Journey time by area

Old town and Port Vauban: about 20 to 30 minutes. Juan-les-Pins: about 25 to 35 minutes. Cap d’Antibes: a little longer, depending on where your villa or hotel is on the peninsula. These times are without traffic: in July and August, the seafront and the area around the Juan-les-Pins pine grove are often busy. The price, however, stays the one shown when you booked.

Useful details for drop-off

Part of Antibes old town is pedestrianised or has restricted access: the chauffeur drops you at the nearest authorised point and helps carry your luggage. For a villa on the Cap d’Antibes, remember to send us the gate code or the caretaker’s number. If you are joining a boat at Port Vauban, tell us the berth, the yacht’s name and the time you are meeting the crew: see also our port and yacht transfers.

The return transfer to the airport

Send us the time of your return flight: we agree the pick-up time together, with a margin suited to the season and the day of the week. By booking the outward and return journeys in one request, everything is settled from the start of your stay. Very early morning and late evening flights are covered, always by advance booking.

The vehicle for your luggage

Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or similar) carries 3 passengers and 3 pieces of luggage. The Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or similar) takes up to 7 passengers and 7 pieces of luggage for a single price, handy for a family with a pushchair or a crew with their kit bags. First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or similar, 3 passengers) and Electric Class (100% electric EQE or EQS) are also available. Baby seats and booster seats on request, to be specified when booking.

During your stay in Antibes

The same service then takes you wherever you wish: dinner in Juan-les-Pins, a day in Cannes or Monaco, a concert at the Jazz à Juan festival in July (Jazz à Juan chauffeur). Frequently asked questions

How long is the transfer from Nice Airport to Antibes?

About 20 to 30 minutes to central Antibes without traffic, a little longer for Juan-les-Pins and the Cap d’Antibes.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

The chauffeur tracks the actual arrival time using your flight number and waits for you as you come out.

Is the price per person?

No, it is per vehicle. It is fixed and shown before you confirm your booking.

Can you drop me in Juan-les-Pins?

Yes. Juan-les-Pins is part of the town of Antibes: just give us the exact address of your accommodation.

Is the transfer available at night?

Yes, 24/7, by advance booking.
