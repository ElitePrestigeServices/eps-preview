Private chauffeur for the Cannes Film Festival

For the Cannes Film Festival in May, Elite Prestige Services, a private chauffeur company based in Cannes, handles your airport transfers, your journeys to the Palais des Festivals and your rides home after the evening, day or night. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and shown before you confirm your booking.

Cannes during the festival

For around ten days, the town runs to the rhythm of the screenings: sections of the Croisette closed, security checks around the Palais des Festivals, queues of cars outside the seafront hotels and heavy demand at closing times. Schedules often change from one hour to the next. A chauffeur based in Cannes knows the usual closures and the drop-off points that actually work, and adapts the route every day.

Arriving in Cannes: Nice and Cannes-Mandelieu airports

From Nice Côte d’Azur Airport, allow about 35 minutes to Cannes outside peak traffic; during the festival, allow more, especially in the late afternoon. Your chauffeur tracks your flight number and meets you at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2. Full details are on our Nice Airport – Cannes transfer page.

For a private jet, Cannes-Mandelieu Airport is about 10 to 20 minutes from the centre of Cannes. See private aviation at Cannes-Mandelieu (in French).

Red carpet, screenings and meetings

Drop-offs close to the Palais depend on your badges, invitations and the traffic rules of the day. Your chauffeur takes you to the nearest authorised point, then agrees a precise pick-up point with you. In evening wear, the Business Van and First Class offer the most comfortable access. Between two screenings, two meetings at the Marché du Film or a lunch on the beach, the car stays available if you have chosen an hourly booking.

Hourly hire, the festival favourite

Hourly hire, on quotation, keeps the same chauffeur and the same car with you for several hours or the whole day. It is the most requested option during the festival: no waiting at the exit, no taxi to hunt for, your belongings safe in the car and a programme that can change. It also suits evenings in villas in the hills above Cannes, in Mougins or on Cap d’Antibes, with a return to your hotel at the end of the night.

Production teams, agencies and delegations

Film crews, distributors, PR agencies or brands present in Cannes: several vehicles can be coordinated for your talent, guests and teams, subject to availability, with a single point of contact. See our corporate and events page.

Which vehicle?

Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or equivalent, 3 passengers, 3 bags) for a solo traveller or a couple. Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or equivalent, 7 passengers, 7 bags) for a team or a delegation. First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or equivalent, 3 passengers) for a more prestigious arrival. Electric Class (100% electric EQE or EQS) on request.

Frequently asked questions

Can the chauffeur drop me in front of the Palais des Festivals?

Only if access is permitted that day. Otherwise, you are dropped at the nearest authorised point and told where you will be collected.

Can I keep the chauffeur for the whole evening?

Yes, with hourly hire: screening, dinner, party, then back to your hotel.

Is the price per person?

No, it is per vehicle. It is fixed and shown before you confirm your booking.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as possible: several weeks before the festival is ideal.

Are you connected with the Festival de Cannes?

No. EPS is an independent transport company, not affiliated with the organisers.
