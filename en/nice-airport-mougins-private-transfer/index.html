Private transfer · Nice Airport · Mougins

Nice Airport to Mougins private transfer with chauffeur

From Nice Côte d’Azur Airport to Mougins, your private chauffeur meets you as you come out of arrivals and takes you straight to your hotel, your villa or the old village, in about 25 to 35 minutes outside peak traffic. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and shown before you confirm your booking. Service available 24/7, by advance booking only.

Book my transfer to Mougins

How the transfer works

When booking, you give us your flight number, terminal, the number of passengers and bags, and your exact address in Mougins. Your chauffeur tracks the actual landing time and adjusts if your flight is early or late. They wait for you in the arrivals hall at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2, help with your luggage, then take the A8 westbound and the hills above Cannes up to your door.

Journey time

Allow about 25 to 35 minutes between the airport and Mougins in normal traffic. At rush hour, on summer Fridays and during major events in Cannes, allow a wider margin, especially for the return. The price is set when you book: it does not change if the road is busier than expected.

Arriving at a villa or in the old village

Many villas and estates in Mougins are reached by private lanes, coded gates or narrow drives. Send us the exact address and the gate code or the caretaker’s contact: your chauffeur prepares the access and drops you as close to the door as possible. The old village is largely pedestrianised: you are dropped at the nearest authorised point, which is close enough for most hotels and restaurants.

Golfers, families and groups

Coming to play one of the golf courses around Mougins? The Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or equivalent, 7 passengers, 7 bags) takes a group and their golf bags for a single price. Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or equivalent, 3 passengers, 3 bags) is ideal for a couple. First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or equivalent, 3 passengers) and Electric Class (100% electric EQE or EQS) are available on request. Baby seats and booster seats on request, to be specified when booking.

The return and other journeys

For your departure, tell us your flight time and we will agree a pick-up time in Mougins with a sensible margin. You can add a stop on the way, or book journeys during your stay: Cannes and the Croisette about ten minutes away, Sophia Antipolis for a meeting, Grasse for a visit. Ideas are on our page on a private chauffeur in Mougins (in French).

During the Cannes Film Festival and trade fairs

During the Cannes Film Festival, Cannes Lions or MIPIM, many visitors stay in Mougins. Book your transfer as soon as your dates are known: availability goes quickly at these times. See also our Nice Airport – Cannes transfer and our page on a chauffeur for the Cannes Film Festival.

Frequently asked questions

How long from Nice Airport to Mougins?

About 25 to 35 minutes outside peak traffic.

Will the chauffeur wait if my flight is late?

Yes, they track the actual landing time using your flight number.

Can I book the outward and return journeys together?

Yes, just include both journeys and times in your request.

Can you provide a child seat?

Yes, please give the children’s ages when booking.

Is the price per person?

No, it is per vehicle. It is fixed and shown before you confirm your booking.
