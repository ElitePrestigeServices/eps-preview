Monaco Yacht Show · Private chauffeur · Port Hercule

Private chauffeur for the Monaco Yacht Show

The Monaco Yacht Show takes place every September at Port Hercule. Elite Prestige Services, a private chauffeur company based in Cannes, arranges your transfers from Nice Airport, Cannes or your hotel, and your days between stands, yacht viewings and business dinners. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and shown before you confirm your booking.

Book my chauffeur for the show

The superyacht show, in the heart of Monaco

For a few days, Port Hercule welcomes brokers, shipyards, designers, buyers and crews around the large yachts moored along the quays. Meetings follow one another: a yacht viewing in the morning, a meeting in a Monte-Carlo hotel, lunch, then an evening on board or in a restaurant in the Principality. Parking is very limited and traffic is heavy when the show opens and closes: with a chauffeur, you arrive on time without looking for a space.

Coming from Nice Airport or Cannes

From Nice Côte d’Azur Airport, allow about 30 minutes to Monaco outside peak traffic; from Cannes, about 55 minutes. During the show, allow a wider margin in the morning and late afternoon. Your chauffeur tracks your flight number and meets you at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2, then chooses between the A8 and the Moyenne Corniche depending on traffic. See also our Nice – Monaco transfer page.

Drop-off near the quays

The area around Port Hercule is reorganised during the event. Your chauffeur drops you at the authorised point closest to the entrance and agrees a precise pick-up point with you. Joining a yacht moored in another harbour, in Cap-d’Ail, Beaulieu or Antibes? Give us the harbour, the quay, the boat’s name and the time agreed with the crew. For other ports on the Riviera, see our port and yacht transfers (in French).

Hourly hire for show days

To fit in several meetings, hourly hire, on quotation, keeps the same chauffeur and the same car with you all day. Your chauffeur stays reachable by phone or WhatsApp, collects you as soon as a viewing ends and keeps your documents or bags in the car. It also works well for accompanying a buyer between several boats, or for a dinner followed by a late return to Cannes or Nice.

Brokers, shipyards and crews

Brokerage teams, exhibiting shipyards and yacht agencies also entrust us with transfers for their clients and crews. Several vehicles can be coordinated at the same time, subject to availability, with a single point of contact and an invoice in the company’s name. See our corporate and events page.

Available vehicles

Business Class: Mercedes E-Class / EQE or equivalent, 3 passengers and 3 bags. Business Van: Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or equivalent, 7 passengers and 7 bags, for a team or a crew with their kit. First Class: Mercedes S-Class / EQS or equivalent, 3 passengers, for hosting a client. Electric Class: 100% electric EQE or EQS.

Booking

Send us your dates, times, addresses and flight numbers via the booking form or WhatsApp, in English or French. You receive a fixed-price quote including VAT by email, with a secure card payment link. September on the Riviera also brings the Cannes Yachting Festival: book as soon as your dates are known.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Monaco Yacht Show?

Every year in September, at Port Hercule. Please check the official dates of each edition with the organiser.

Can the chauffeur drop me on the quay?

You are dropped at the authorised point closest to the show entrance.

Can you transport a crew?

Yes, the Business Van takes up to 7 people with their luggage; several vehicles are possible subject to availability.

Is the price per person?

No, it is per vehicle. It is fixed and shown before you confirm your booking.

Is EPS a partner of the show?

No, EPS is an independent transport company, not affiliated with the organiser.
