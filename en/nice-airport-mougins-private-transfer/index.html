Private transfer · Nice Airport · Mougins

Nice Airport to Mougins private transfer with chauffeur

From Nice Côte d’Azur Airport to Mougins, your private chauffeur meets you in the arrivals hall and drives you to your hotel, your villa or the foot of the old village in about 25 to 35 minutes outside peak traffic. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and shown before you confirm your booking. Service available 24/7, by advance booking only.

Your arrival, step by step

When booking, you give us your flight number, your terminal (Terminal 1 or Terminal 2), the number of passengers and bags, and your exact address in Mougins. Your chauffeur tracks the actual landing time: if your plane lands early or late, they adjust without you having to let them know.

As you come out, they greet you in the arrivals hall, take care of your luggage and walk you to the car. The route follows the A8 westbound, then the roads that climb into the hills above Cannes. No queue and no change along the way.

A private chauffeur rather than a taxi or shuttle

Mougins is not an easy place to reach by public transport with suitcases: residential areas are spread across the hills and many addresses are far from any stop. A shared shuttle makes detours, and a taxi from the rank may not know how to reach your property. With a chauffeur booked in advance, you know the price before you leave, your chauffeur already has your address and instructions, and they wait for you whatever time your flight actually lands.

Villas, estates and the old village

Some villas in Mougins are reached by private lanes, gates with access codes or narrow driveways. Include the code, the caretaker’s name or your concierge’s contact details with your request: your chauffeur plans the arrival and drops you as close to the door as possible.

The old village sits on a hilltop and is largely pedestrianised, so drop-off is at the nearest authorised point.

Useful journey times

Nice Airport → Mougins: about 25 to 35 minutes outside peak traffic. Mougins → Cannes and La Croisette: about ten minutes. At rush hour, on summer Fridays and during the major trade fairs in Cannes, the A8 can be busy: we take this into account when setting the pick-up time for your return. The price stays the one confirmed when you booked.

Golf, families, groups: which vehicle?

Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or similar, 3 passengers, 3 bags) suits a solo traveller or a couple. The Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or similar, 7 passengers, 7 bags) takes a family or a group of golfers with their bags, for a single price per vehicle. First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or similar, 3 passengers) offers a higher level of comfort. Electric Class (fully electric EQE or EQS) is charged at the Business Class rate in the EQE and the First Class rate in the EQS. Baby seats and boosters on request, to be specified when booking.

Arriving by private jet or by train

Flying private? Cannes-Mandelieu Airport is another gateway to Mougins: see our page on private aviation at Cannes-Mandelieu (in French). Arriving by train? Pick-up at Cannes station is available: give us your train number and your chauffeur will track its actual arrival time.

Your return and journeys during your stay

For your departure, tell us your flight time and we will agree a pick-up time in Mougins with a suitable margin. Book both journeys in a single request; each one is confirmed at a fixed price. During your stay, the same service can take you to dinner in Cannes, a meeting in Sophia Antipolis or a visit to Grasse. For a sightseeing day, hourly hire is available on quotation: see our French Riviera excursions with chauffeur (in French) and our private chauffeur in Mougins page (in French).

During the Film Festival and trade fairs in Cannes

During the Cannes Film Festival, Cannes Lions, MIPIM or MIPCOM, many attendees choose to stay in Mougins, away from the bustle yet close to the Palais des Festivals. Book your transfer as soon as your dates are set, as availability goes quickly at these times. See also our Cannes Film Festival chauffeur page.

Frequently asked questions

How long from Nice Airport to Mougins?

About 25 to 35 minutes outside peak traffic, via the A8.

Will the chauffeur wait if my flight is late?

Yes, they track the actual landing time using your flight number.

Can you reach a villa with a gate?

Yes, just send us the code or the caretaker’s contact details when booking.

Can you carry golf bags?

Yes, choose the Business Van and tell us how many bags you have.

Is the price per person?

No, it is per vehicle. It is fixed and shown before you confirm your booking.
