Cannes Lions Private Chauffeur

Cannes Lions brings thousands of agency, brand and media professionals to the Croisette every June, and the days run from early breakfasts to late beach parties. A private chauffeur based in Cannes keeps your schedule on track: airport arrivals, transfers between hotels and the Palais des Festivals, client dinners in the hills and the ride back after the last event, at any hour.

Book your Cannes Lions chauffeur →

Nice Airport arrivals with flight tracking

Your chauffeur follows your flight in real time and meets you in the arrivals hall at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 of Nice Côte d’Azur Airport, with a name sign if you wish. In normal traffic, the drive to Cannes takes about 35 to 45 minutes; during festival week, allow extra time on the coastal roads and around the town centre. Transfers from Cannes-Mandelieu Airport are also available for private flights.

Getting around the Palais and the Croisette

Traffic on the Croisette is heavily regulated during Cannes Lions, and some streets near the Palais des Festivals may be closed or restricted. Your chauffeur drops you at the closest authorised point and agrees a precise pick-up spot with you, so you are not left searching for your car between sessions. For agencies hosting guests, we can coordinate several pick-ups from different hotels.

Hourly service for full days

For a packed agenda, book a chauffeur at your disposal by the hour: the vehicle waits nearby while you attend talks, meetings and award ceremonies, then takes you on to a dinner in Mougins or a party at a beach club. This is often simpler than booking separate rides when your timetable keeps changing.

Choosing your vehicle

Business Class (Mercedes E-Class or equivalent) seats up to 3 passengers and suits a solo traveller or a couple with standard luggage. Business Van (Mercedes V-Class) carries up to 7 passengers with room for large suitcases, golf bags or equipment. First Class (Mercedes S-Class or equivalent) is available on request for VIP guests and special occasions. All vehicles are driven by professional, discreet chauffeurs.

Price and booking

The price is fixed, per vehicle, and shown before you confirm your booking.

Requests sent early have the best availability.

Frequently asked questions

Is Elite Prestige Services an official Cannes Lions partner?

No. We are an independent chauffeur company and are not affiliated with the organisers. Please check official dates and venue access rules on the festival website.

Can I book several journeys in one request?

Yes. List all your rides, times and addresses in the booking form, or send them via WhatsApp, and you will receive one consolidated fixed-price quote.

What if my flight is delayed?

Flight tracking means your chauffeur adjusts to your actual landing time, so you do not need to call ahead.
