Getting to Saint-Tropez with a chauffeur

Saint-Tropez has no railway station and no commercial airport close by: most travellers arrive via Nice Airport or Cannes. The route follows the A8 and then the roads of the Var, which are often congested in summer. A private chauffeur spares you the drive and the hunt for a parking space in a very busy village.

We collect you in Cannes, at Nice Airport, in Antibes, in Fréjus / Saint-Raphaël or at your hotel, and drop you outside your villa or restaurant.

Journeys to Saint-Tropez

Typical journey times, without traffic:

Cannes ↔ Saint-Tropez: about 1 hr 30 min without traffic.

Nice Airport ↔ Saint-Tropez: price on request, depending on the exact address in the gulf. See our Nice Airport – Saint-Tropez transfer page.

Monaco, Antibes, Gassin, Grimaud or a long-distance journey to Toulon or Marseille: price on request.

Around the gulf: Pampelonne, Ramatuelle, Place des Lices

Once you are there, your chauffeur takes you wherever you need to go: the beaches of Pampelonne, the villas of Ramatuelle and Gassin, the Old Port, Place des Lices on market days, or dinner in the village. For a full day or an evening, a chauffeur at your disposal is available on request: the chauffeur stays with you, saving you the back-and-forth.

Les Voiles de Saint-Tropez and the summer season

July and August are the busiest months: the access road is often slow, especially in the late afternoon and evening. Allow extra time and book early. In autumn, Les Voiles de Saint-Tropez draws crews and spectators to the harbour; we provide transfers between your hotel, the port and the airport. See chauffeur for Les Voiles de Saint-Tropez.

Arriving by sea? We will meet you at the harbour with your luggage: port transfers.

Which vehicle for Saint-Tropez?

For a family holiday or a group of friends with plenty of luggage, the Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or similar) carries 7 passengers and 7 pieces of luggage for a single price. For two, Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or similar, 3 passengers, 3 pieces of luggage) or First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or similar, 3 passengers) makes the drive more comfortable. Electric Class (100% electric Mercedes EQE / EQS): same price as Business Class for the EQE and as First Class for the EQS. Baby seats and booster seats available on request.
