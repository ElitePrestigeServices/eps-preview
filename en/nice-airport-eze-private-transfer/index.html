Nice Airport to Èze private transfer with chauffeur

To reach Èze village, Èze-sur-Mer or a villa on the Corniche from Nice Côte d’Azur Airport, Elite Prestige Services meets you as you come out of arrivals and drives you to your door in about 25 to 35 minutes outside peak traffic. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and shown before you confirm your booking. Private hire (VTC) transfer available 24/7, by advance booking only.

Your arrival at Nice Airport

When booking, give us your flight number, the terminal if you know it and your exact address in Èze. Your chauffeur tracks the actual landing time: if the plane is early or late, they adjust without you having to call. They wait for you in the arrivals hall of Terminal 1 or Terminal 2, take your luggage and walk you to the car. Within minutes of collecting your cases, you are on the road to Èze.

Èze village or Èze-sur-Mer: which route?

Èze has two quite separate parts. Èze village, perched on its rock high above the sea, is reached by the Moyenne Corniche, with open views over the bay. Èze-sur-Mer, down by the water, is reached by the Basse Corniche, past Villefranche-sur-Mer and Beaulieu-sur-Mer. Your chauffeur chooses the route according to your address and the traffic at the time. Allow about 25 to 35 minutes either way; at rush hour, crossing Nice can take longer, with no change to the agreed price.

Rather than a taxi or a bus

Bus routes link Nice and Èze, but with stops, sometimes a change and little room for suitcases. At the airport, a taxi from the rank will also take you there, but with no price known in advance and no personal welcome. With a booked private chauffeur, the car is ready when you land, the route is planned around your address and you know the price before you confirm.

Arriving in a pedestrianised village

The medieval heart of Èze village is for pedestrians only: steep lanes, steps and vaulted passages climb up to the exotic garden at the top of the rock. The car therefore stops at the entrance to the village. Tell us the name of your hotel or rental: we drop you at the most convenient access point, and your chauffeur helps you unload your luggage. If you are travelling with a lot, keep a bag that is easy to carry for the walk up.

Villas on the Corniche

Between Èze, Beaulieu-sur-Mer and the Monaco border, many villas sit at the end of narrow lanes or behind a gate. Give us the precise address, the access code or the caretaker’s number: your chauffeur arrives at the right entrance and leaves you at the door. For a family or a group of friends with plenty of luggage, the Business Van is the most comfortable choice. Arriving by private jet? See our page on private aviation at Nice Airport (in French).

Which vehicle?

Business Class: Mercedes E-Class / EQE or equivalent, 3 passengers, 3 bags, ideal for a couple. Business Van: Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or equivalent, 7 passengers, 7 bags. First Class: Mercedes S-Class / EQS or equivalent, 3 passengers, the smoothest ride on the bends of the Corniche. Electric Class: 100% electric EQE or EQS. Baby seats and booster seats on request, to be specified when booking.

Monaco and Cap-Ferrat close by

From Èze, Monaco is about 15 to 20 minutes away: your chauffeur can take you there for dinner or an evening at the Casino de Monte-Carlo, then bring you back. Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat and Villefranche-sur-Mer are also very close. For getting around during your stay, see our page on a private chauffeur in Èze (in French); if part of your group is staying in the Principality, see the Nice – Monaco transfer.

The return to the airport

For your return flight, we agree the departure time from Èze together, taking into account your flight time, terminal and traffic on the Promenade des Anglais or the A8. Your chauffeur picks you up at the same point as on arrival. The service also runs for very early morning and late evening flights.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take from Nice Airport to Èze?

About 25 to 35 minutes outside peak traffic, for Èze village and Èze-sur-Mer alike.

Can the chauffeur drop me outside my hotel in the village?

No, the centre is pedestrianised: you are dropped at the entrance to the village, at the closest accessible point.

What if my flight is delayed?

Your chauffeur tracks your flight by its number and adjusts the pick-up time.

Can we stop in Monaco or Beaulieu-sur-Mer on the way?

Yes, list your stops when booking so they are included in the fixed price.

Is the price per person?

No, it is per vehicle. It is fixed and shown before you confirm your booking.
