Private Chauffeur for the Cannes Film Festival

Every May, the Cannes Film Festival turns the Croisette into one of the busiest places in Europe. Between red-carpet screenings, press appointments, market meetings and late-night parties, a private chauffeur based in Cannes gives you reliable transport at any hour, with a driver who knows the town, its closures and its shortcuts.

Airport arrivals during the festival

Your chauffeur monitors your flight and waits in the arrivals hall at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 of Nice Côte d’Azur Airport. In normal traffic, Cannes is about 35 to 45 minutes away; during the festival, delays around the town centre are common, so allow extra time before an evening screening. Private flights are welcomed at Cannes-Mandelieu Airport, a short drive from the Croisette.

Drop-off near the Palais des Festivals

Access to the Palais des Festivals and the Croisette is tightly controlled during the festival, and some sections are closed to traffic. Your chauffeur takes you to the nearest authorised drop-off point and agrees an exact meeting place for your return. Red-carpet access itself depends on your accreditation and the organisers’ rules.

Hourly service for screenings and evenings

For a day of meetings followed by a gala or a party in the hills, an hourly booking is the most flexible option. The car waits nearby, you change plans freely, and your chauffeur takes you home whatever the time. We also arrange evening trips to Mougins, Antibes or Cap d’Antibes.

Choosing your vehicle

Business Class (Mercedes E-Class or equivalent) seats up to 3 passengers and suits a solo traveller or a couple with standard luggage. Business Van (Mercedes V-Class) carries up to 7 passengers with room for large suitcases, golf bags or equipment. First Class (Mercedes S-Class or equivalent) is available on request for VIP guests and special occasions. For evening wear and long gowns, First Class and Business Van offer the most comfortable access.

Festival prices

Outside major events, Nice Airport to Cannes starts from €130 in Business Class, €160 in Business Van and €200 in First Class, per vehicle, not per person. During the festival a demand-related surcharge applies; the fixed price is confirmed before you book. Availability is limited, so early requests are recommended.

Frequently asked questions

Are you linked to the Festival de Cannes?

No. We are an independent chauffeur company, not affiliated with the festival. Check the official dates on the festival website.

Can the chauffeur wait during a screening?

Yes, with an hourly booking. The car waits nearby and collects you at the agreed point.

How do I contact my chauffeur on the night?

Your booking confirmation includes the details, and you can reach us on WhatsApp.
