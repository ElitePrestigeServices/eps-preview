Nice Airport – Cannes transfer

Electric Class (100% electric Mercedes EQE / EQS): same price as Business Class for the EQE and as First Class for the EQS.

A pick-up timed to your flight

When booking, give us your flight number and, if you know it, the terminal (T1 or T2). The chauffeur tracks your actual landing time: if your flight is delayed or early, the pick-up is adjusted accordingly. The exact meeting arrangements are sent to you with your confirmation.

For your return journey, tell us your flight time: together we will set a departure time from Cannes so that you reach the airport comfortably.

The journey between the airport and Cannes

The route follows the A8 and takes about 35 minutes without traffic. At peak times, in summer or during the major Cannes trade shows, it can take longer. The price stays fixed: it is confirmed in your quote before departure, however long the drive takes.

We drop off at any address in Cannes: hotels on the Croisette, the Palais des Festivals, Le Suquet, La Californie, the Old Port, Port Canto. Staying in Le Cannet, Mougins or Mandelieu-la-Napoule? Ask us for a quote. See also our private chauffeur in Mougins page.

Families, groups and luggage

Travelling with children? Let us know when booking how many baby seats or booster seats you need: they are provided on request. From 4 passengers, or with a lot of suitcases, choose the Business Van: up to 7 passengers and 7 pieces of luggage, for a single price per vehicle.

Private flight or arrival at Cannes-Mandelieu

If you are flying in on a business aviation flight, see our private aviation at Nice Airport page. VAT: see private aviation Cannes-Mandelieu.

How to book your transfer

Fill in the form or message us on WhatsApp: date, flight number, number of passengers and pieces of luggage, and your address in Cannes. You will receive a fixed-price quote including VAT by email, followed by a secure card payment link (Stripe). The service runs 24/7, by advance booking.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a transfer from Nice Airport to Cannes cost?

The price is fixed, per vehicle, and shown before you confirm your booking.

How long does the journey take?

About 35 minutes without traffic via the A8.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

The chauffeur tracks your actual arrival time using your flight number and adjusts accordingly.

Does the price depend on the number of passengers?

No, it depends on the vehicle. Up to 7 passengers travel in the Business Van for a single price.

Can I book for a flight arriving at night?

Yes, we operate 24/7, by advance booking.
