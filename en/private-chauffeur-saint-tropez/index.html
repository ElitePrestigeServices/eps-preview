Reaching Saint-Tropez without driving

Saint-Tropez sits at the end of a peninsula, well away from the main roads. Most visitors land at Nice Côte d’Azur Airport, come from Cannes or arrive by train at Saint-Raphaël station. The last part of the journey follows narrow Var roads that are very busy in season: with a chauffeur, there is no hire car to return and no parking space to hunt for in the village.

Rather than a taxi found on the spot or a shuttle shared with other passengers, a private hire vehicle booked in advance gives you a car for your party alone, a price known before you set off and a chauffeur who knows the exact address of your villa or hotel.

Journey times to Saint-Tropez

Typical times, without traffic:

Cannes → Saint-Tropez: about 1 h 30.

Nice Airport → Saint-Tropez: about 1 h 30 to 2 h, depending on the address in the gulf. Full details of the airport meet-and-greet are on our Nice Airport – Saint-Tropez transfer page.

Saint-Raphaël station, Fréjus, Antibes or Monaco: pick-up at the address of your choice, with a fixed price shown before you confirm. Long-distance journeys to Toulon, Marseille or Italy are priced on request.

In July and August, traffic into Saint-Tropez often slows in late morning and late afternoon. If you have a flight or train to catch on the way back, we agree a departure time together with a comfortable margin.

Getting around the gulf

Once there, your chauffeur drops you as close as possible to each place: the beaches of Pampelonne on the Ramatuelle side, a villa in the hills of Gassin, the Old Port for dinner facing the boats, or the Place des Lices on market mornings. The village streets are narrow and sometimes closed to cars, so drop-off is at the nearest authorised point, with help for your luggage.

For an evening out, you can keep the same chauffeur from dinner until you are back at your accommodation. A chauffeur at your disposal by the hour or by the day is available on request: useful for moving between beach, lunch and appointments without waiting for a car in between.

Day trips from Saint-Tropez

Your stay can also be a chance to explore the French Riviera: a day in Cannes on the Croisette, a return trip to Monaco and the Casino de Monte-Carlo, or a drive through the Estérel and Saint-Raphaël. The chauffeur drives and waits for you; he is not a guide, but he knows the access roads and drop-off points. Ideas for routes on our French Riviera chauffeur excursions page.

Les Voiles de Saint-Tropez and arrivals by sea

In autumn, Les Voiles de Saint-Tropez brings crews and spectators to the harbour: arrivals and departures are concentrated over a few days, so it is best to book early. See our Les Voiles de Saint-Tropez chauffeur page. Joining a yacht or leaving one? Tell us the port, the berth and the name of the boat: we will be waiting with your luggage.

Which vehicle should you choose?

For two or three people, Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or similar, 3 passengers, 3 pieces of luggage) suits most journeys. For a family or a group of friends, the Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or similar) carries 7 passengers and 7 pieces of luggage for a single price. First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or similar, 3 passengers) offers more space for the drive from Nice. Electric Class (100% electric EQE or EQS) is also available. Baby seats and booster seats on request, to be specified when booking.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take from Cannes to Saint-Tropez?

About 1 h 30 without traffic. In high summer, allow more time, especially at peak arrival times on the peninsula.

How much is a private chauffeur to Saint-Tropez?

The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person. It is shown in the booking form before you confirm.

Can you collect me from Saint-Raphaël station?

Yes. Give us your train number and the chauffeur will adjust to the actual arrival time.

Can I keep the chauffeur for the evening?

Yes, with a chauffeur at your disposal, priced on request. The chauffeur stays reachable and drives you back whenever you choose.

Do you operate at night?

Yes, 24/7, always by advance booking.
