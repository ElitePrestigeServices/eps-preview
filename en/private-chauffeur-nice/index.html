Private chauffeur and driver service in Nice

Landing at Nice Côte d’Azur Airport or staying on the Promenade des Anglais? Elite Prestige Services provides chauffeur-driven journeys within Nice and across the whole Riviera: Nice Airport to Cannes from 130 € incl. VAT, to Monaco from 150 € incl. VAT, per vehicle. Available 24/7, by advance booking, with a fixed price agreed before you set off.

Nice Côte d’Azur Airport: Terminal 1 and Terminal 2

Nice Airport is where most of our clients arrive. We pick up at both terminals, T1 and T2. Just give us your flight number: the chauffeur tracks your actual arrival time and adjusts if you land early or late.

For private flights, business aviation arrivals and departures are covered on our private aviation at Nice Airport page.

Popular journeys from Nice and starting prices

Prices from, VAT included, per vehicle (Business Class / Business Van / First Class):

Nice Airport ↔ Cannes: 130 € / 160 € / 200 €, about 35 min without traffic.

Nice Airport ↔ Monaco: 150 € / 195 € / 240 €, about 30 min without traffic.

From central Nice, Old Nice, the port or Nice-Ville station to Antibes, Èze, Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat, Menton or Saint-Tropez, the price is provided on request. Long-distance journeys to Marseille, Toulon or Italy are also quoted on request.

Around Nice: hotels, port and station

We serve the hotels on the Promenade des Anglais, addresses in Old Nice and the Carré d’Or, the hills of Cimiez and Mont Boron, the port of Nice and Nice-Ville station. If you are arriving by train, give us the train number: the chauffeur tracks its actual arrival time, just as for a flight.

Hotels, concierge services and agencies in Nice: we offer a fixed rate all year round, with no event surcharge, and a booking link in your name. See hotels and concierge services.

Nice, your gateway to the Riviera

From Nice, the Moyenne Corniche leads to Èze and Monaco, while the A8 motorway takes you to Antibes, Cannes and the Var. Many clients fly into Nice for a stay in Monaco or a meeting in Cannes. You can also book a one-way journey today and your return to the airport at the end of your stay.

In July and August, prices are increased by 15%. During the region’s major events (Cannes Film Festival, Monaco Grand Prix, MIPIM, Cannes Lions, Yachting Festival), an event surcharge applies: only the higher of the two is charged, never both.

The right vehicle for your stay in Nice

Travelling alone or as a couple with little luggage, Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or similar, 3 passengers, 3 pieces of luggage) is all you need. For families with pushchairs, suitcases or golf equipment, the Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or similar) takes 7 passengers and 7 pieces of luggage. For VIP clients, First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or similar, 3 passengers). For a 100% electric journey, Electric Class (Mercedes EQE / EQS), price on request. Baby seats and booster seats available on request.

Frequently asked questions

Will the chauffeur wait if my flight lands late in Nice?

Yes. Using your flight number, the chauffeur tracks your actual landing time at T1 or T2.

How much is a chauffeur from Nice Airport to Monaco?

From 150 € incl. VAT (Business Class), 195 € (Business Van) or 240 € (First Class) for the whole vehicle.

Do you offer journeys within Nice itself?

Yes, between a hotel, the station, the port or a private address. These journeys are quoted on request.

Can 6 passengers travel together with suitcases?

Yes, in the Business Van: up to 7 passengers and 7 pieces of luggage, for a single price per vehicle.

Do you speak English?

Yes, we answer your requests in French or English.
