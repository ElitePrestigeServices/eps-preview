FRENCH RIVIERA EVENTS CALENDAR

Major events in Cannes, Nice, Monaco, Saint-Tropez, Antibes, Fréjus and Saint-Raphaël

Festivals, conferences, trade shows, concerts, sporting and sailing events: discover the Riviera’s recurring highlights and plan your chauffeur-driven transfer. Months are for guidance only: some events do not take place every year or change dates.

Cannes

Main recurring events and cultural highlights in Cannes.

JanuaryMIDEM / music events (depending on edition)

Professional and music events in Cannes, with transfers between hotels, the Palais des Festivals and the airports.

FebruaryFestival International des Jeux (Cannes Games Festival)

Large crowds around the Palais des Festivals and Cannes town centre.

MarchMIPIM, Canneseries (month varies)

The international property congress and audiovisual events: business transfers and chauffeur-at-disposal hire.

AprilCanneseries, MIPDOC (depending on edition)

Journeys between hotels, screenings, business meetings and the Palais des Festivals.

MayCannes Film Festival, Marché du Film

A period of very high demand for airport transfers, hotels, evening events and chauffeur-at-disposal hire.

JuneCannes Lions, Midem Live (depending on edition)

International conferences and events with intense chauffeur activity in Cannes and along the Croisette.

JulyNuits Musicales du Suquet, summer concerts

Concerts and summer evenings, with pick-ups available from hotels, villas and restaurants.

July–AugustFestival d’Art Pyrotechnique (Cannes Fireworks Festival)

Very busy evenings along the Cannes seafront, with tighter traffic and parking conditions.

SeptemberCannes Yachting Festival

Transfers for exhibitors and visitors between hotels, ports and Nice Airport during the boat show.

September–OctoberTFWA World Exhibition

A major international trade event at the Palais des Festivals and in Cannes hotels.

OctoberMIPCOM, IGTM

Trade conferences generating numerous transfers between the airport, hotels and meeting venues.

NovemberMAPIC

The international retail property show at the Palais des Festivals.

DecemberILTM Cannes, Christmas festivities

ILTM brings together a high-end international clientele; December also hosts the festive season celebrations.

Nice

Main recurring events and major sporting and cultural highlights in Nice.

JanuaryProm’Classic

A road race on the Promenade des Anglais, with traffic changes and large crowds on race day.

FebruaryNice Carnival

Parades, Flower Battles and festivities draw an international crowd for several weeks.

MarchParis–Nice cycle race finish

A major cycling event with temporary traffic restrictions in some areas.

April / MayNice International Half Marathon

A major sporting event on the Promenade des Anglais and the seafront.

JuneIRONMAN Nice / IRONMAN 70.3

An international triathlon drawing large crowds, with restrictions on several main roads.

JulyNice Jazz Fest

A major music festival, with transfers between the airport, hotels, restaurants and concert venues.

SeptemberUTMB Nice Côte d’Azur

Trail races linking Nice with its hinterland, with transport needs over several days.

NovemberNice–Cannes International Marathon

A major sporting event between Nice and Cannes, with many road closures and diversions.

May–JuneFormula 1 Monaco Grand Prix

Summer concerts and evening events around Monte-Carlo, its hotels and reception venues.

SeptemberMonaco Yacht Show, Monaco Classic Week (depending on edition)

Major yachting events linking hotels, Port Hercule, the airport and the heliport.

NovemberNo Finish Line Monaco

A recurring charity sporting event in the Principality.

DecemberChristmas Market, festive season celebrations

Seasonal festivities and end-of-year evenings in Monaco.
