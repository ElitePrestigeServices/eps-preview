Airport transfer · Nice · Monaco

Nice Airport to Monaco Private Chauffeur

Elite Prestige Services links Nice Côte d’Azur Airport and Monaco in about 30 minutes without traffic. Your chauffeur tracks your flight, meets you at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 and drops you in Monte-Carlo, Fontvieille, at Port Hercule or outside your residence, and the journey works the other way too. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and is shown before you confirm your booking.

The Nice Airport – Monaco journey

Allow about 30 minutes without traffic between the airport and the Principality. The chauffeur picks the route according to the time of day and the traffic: the A8, the most direct, or the Moyenne Corniche, which runs above Èze with the sea below. The price is confirmed before departure and does not change if the roads are busier than expected. Staying in Èze on the way? See our Nice Airport – Èze transfer.

Meeting you at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2

Give us your flight number when booking: the chauffeur follows your actual landing time and adjusts if you are early or late. The meeting arrangements are sent with your confirmation. The service runs 24/7, for the first morning flights as well as night-time arrivals.

Arriving in Monaco: districts and access

In Monaco, stopping outside a hotel or a building means knowing the access points: one-way streets, underground entrances, public lifts. Your chauffeur drops you as close as possible in Monte-Carlo around the Casino, at Larvotto, in La Condamine by Port Hercule, in Fontvieille or outside your residence. Send us the exact address and, for a yacht, the quay and the name of the boat.

From central Nice or Nice-Ville station

The journey to Monaco can also be booked from a hotel on the Promenade des Anglais, the Old Town, the port of Nice or Nice-Ville station: the price is then set by quote. For your other journeys in Nice, see our private chauffeur in Nice page.

Grand Prix, Monaco Yacht Show and major events

During the Monaco Grand Prix, many streets are closed and drop-offs take place at set points: book as soon as your dates are known, see our Monaco Grand Prix chauffeur page. In September, during the Monaco Yacht Show at Port Hercule, we arrange transfers between the airport, your hotel and the quays. On gala or conference days, remember to book your return as well.

Taxi, shuttle, train or private chauffeur to Monaco?

Between Nice Airport and Monaco you can take a taxi from the rank, a shuttle or a bus, the train from Nice-Ville, a helicopter, or book a private hire chauffeur. With luggage, a private transfer remains the simplest option: no connections, no waiting at the rank, and a drop-off at your door. For 4 or 5 travellers, a single Business Van is enough, for a single price per vehicle.

Families, luggage and vehicles

Baby seats and booster seats are provided on request; just mention it when booking. Business Class: Mercedes E-Class / EQE or similar, 3 passengers and 3 pieces of luggage. Business Van: Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or similar, 7 passengers and 7 pieces of luggage. First Class: Mercedes S-Class / EQS or similar, 3 passengers. Electric Class: 100% electric EQE or EQS, at the Business Class price for the EQE and the First Class price for the EQS.

From Monaco back to the airport

Tell us your flight time: together we agree a pick-up time in Monaco, with a margin suited to the day. For a stay of several days, your chauffeur can also drive you to dinners and evenings out: see our private chauffeur in Monaco page.

Booking your Nice – Monaco transfer

Use the website form or WhatsApp, in English or in French: date, flight number, passengers, luggage and address in Monaco. You receive a fixed-price quote including VAT by email and a secure card payment link.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a transfer from Nice Airport to Monaco cost?

You pay one fixed price for the whole vehicle, whatever the number of passengers, and it appears in the booking form before you confirm.

How long does the journey take?

About 30 minutes without traffic from the airport, via the A8 or the Moyenne Corniche.

Will the chauffeur wait if my flight is late?

Yes, the chauffeur tracks your actual arrival time using your flight number.

Do you drive from central Nice?

Yes, from a hotel, Nice-Ville station or a private address, by quote.

Is the transfer possible during the Grand Prix?

Yes, taking the street closures into account. Book as early as possible.

Planning a journey?

Send your request in two minutes: you receive a fixed-price quote before confirming.
