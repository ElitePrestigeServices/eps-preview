Nice Airport to Saint-Tropez private transfer with chauffeur

A private chauffeur meets you at Nice Côte d’Azur Airport and drives you straight to Saint-Tropez, Ramatuelle, Gassin or anywhere else in the gulf, in about 1 hr 30 to 2 hrs outside traffic. Elite Prestige Services is a licensed private hire (VTC) company based in Cannes, available 24/7 on advance booking. The price is fixed, per vehicle rather than per person, and is shown before you confirm your booking.

Book my transfer to Saint-Tropez

Why a private transfer to the gulf?

Saint-Tropez has no railway station, and Nice Airport is around a hundred kilometres away. By public transport the journey often means several changes, with luggage to carry. A taxi from the rank does not always quote the fare in advance. With a pre-booked transfer, you travel in one go, door to door, and the price is known before you leave. After a long flight, with family or with plenty of luggage, it is the simplest option.

Your arrival, step by step

Before your flight: you give us your flight number, the terminal if you know it and the exact address of your hotel or villa. On landing: the chauffeur tracks your actual arrival time, at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2, and adjusts if you are early or late. In the hall: he waits for you in arrivals, takes care of your luggage and walks you to the car. On the road: a direct drive to your door, with a stop if you requested one when booking.

The route from Nice Airport to Saint-Tropez

The chauffeur takes the A8 motorway westwards, passes the Esterel on the motorway, then reaches the gulf on the roads of the Var. Outside traffic, allow about 1 hr 30 to 2 hrs. In July and August, and in the late afternoon, traffic into the gulf slows down considerably and the journey can take much longer. The route is adapted to the time and traffic, and the price agreed at booking stays the same if the road is slower.

Where can we drop you in the gulf?

Saint-Tropez: town-centre hotels, the harbour, the Place des Lices area, addresses near the Citadel. Ramatuelle: villas and venues close to Pampelonne beaches. Gassin, Grimaud and Port Grimaud: estates, residences and houses in the hills. Cogolin and Sainte-Maxime: hotels and holiday rentals. Many properties have a gate, a code or a private lane: please mention it when booking so we can drop you off without detours.

Private jet, Cannes or Marseille

Arriving by business aviation? The meeting point is confirmed in advance, see private aviation at Nice Airport. From Cannes, allow about 1 hr 30 to Saint-Tropez. Landing at Marseille Provence instead? A transfer to the gulf is available on quotation: see Marseille Airport – French Riviera.

Choosing your vehicle for this long drive

For a couple or a business traveller, Business Class (Mercedes E-Class / EQE or similar) seats 3 passengers with 3 pieces of luggage. For a family, friends or golf bags, the Business Van (Mercedes V-Class Extra-Long or similar) takes up to 7 passengers and 7 pieces of luggage, for one price per vehicle. First Class (Mercedes S-Class / EQS or similar, 3 passengers) offers the most room to rest during the journey. Electric Class (100% electric EQE or EQS) is also available. Baby seats and booster seats on request, to be specified when booking.

The return to Nice Airport

For your departure, give us your flight time: the pick-up time is set with enough margin to leave the gulf, especially on a summer Saturday. Booking both ways at once saves any last-minute search. During your stay, a chauffeur can also take you to restaurants, beaches or on an excursion: see private chauffeur in Saint-Tropez and, in autumn, Les Voiles de Saint-Tropez.

Booking

Use the booking form or WhatsApp, in French or English: date, flight number, passengers, luggage and your address in the gulf. You receive a quote at a fixed price including VAT by email, with a secure card payment link.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the drive from Nice Airport to Saint-Tropez?

About 1 hr 30 to 2 hrs outside traffic. In high summer and late in the day, allow more time.

Does the price go up if there are traffic jams?

No. The price is fixed, per vehicle, and is shown before you confirm your booking.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

The chauffeur tracks your flight number and waits for your actual arrival time.

Can you drop us in Ramatuelle or Port Grimaud?

Yes, anywhere in the gulf. Please give the exact address and any access codes.

Is it less convenient than a taxi or a shuttle?

A private hire car is booked in advance: no queue, no changes and a price known from the moment you book.
